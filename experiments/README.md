# Experiments

Experiment entry points are grouped by CounterFlow research axis.

```text
experiments/research_axes/latent_update_method/
  CounterFlow sampler, guidance, transition, solver, latent trajectory experiments.

experiments/research_axes/target_prompt_generation/
  Reserved for prompt-bank experiment entry points when they become larger than scripts.

experiments/research_axes/evaluation_metrics/
  Baseline dispatches and metric/protocol comparison experiments.
```

Keep new experiment entry points under the matching axis directory.
